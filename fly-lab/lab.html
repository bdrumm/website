REWARD RULES

Food goals give the main reward. New areas give a small, one-time exploration reward. Larger outcomes produce longer reinforcement pulses; the deadband ignores very small net outcomes. Use frozen-memory runs with the same seed to compare behavior.

Auto uses a bus harness for driving and an operator chair for game and website tasks. Arena trials use free walking.

PERMITTED LEGS

Bus steering and propulsion use front legs plus the right middle pedal leg. Panel tasks use front legs to choose controls and the right middle leg to activate. Reverse also uses the left middle leg to shift gear. On websites, the left middle leg operates Back and the rear legs scroll up/down. Restraints may block a neural request.

Body locks are relative to the seat. The bus can move while the fly stays secured. This operator model is engineered, not a validated fly musculoskeletal simulation. Motor activity continues under restraint; a blocked task request does not silence neurons.

Inside the simulation

In the browser, choose an arena preset and start the demo. Use W, A, D and S, or the action buttons, to advance one decision at a time. Your actions drive the environment directly; the browser does not simulate neural activity or learning.

  1. Design: click a placement tool, then click the arena. Tune rewards and episode settings under Training.
  2. With compute: memory persists across episodes. Turn learning off for a frozen-memory comparison.
  3. With compute: pause at a decision boundary, advance one decision, and compare behavior with the exact sensory input and measured neural rates.
  4. With compute: save the brain, then choose its checkpoint under Training to start a new episode with that memory.

The fly moves on a plane displayed in 3D. Its body and wings illustrate behavior; this is not a biomechanical flight model. The sensory projection, behavior readout, and reward rules are engineered interfaces. Reward or changed weights alone do not establish learning.

Browser scenario saves stay in this browser. Configuration and demo summaries can be exported. The browser runs one manual arena episode; randomized food, other task execution, neural training, assays and checkpoints require compute. Other task scenes show their default layout.
