Features

Contoured shell
Rounded outer edges and a smooth, rib-free interior follow the shape of the baguette.
Flush internal hinge
A compact print-in-place hinge sits within the shell, with a lid that opens to 100°.
Two integrated latches
Two inward-set latches hold tension against their catches, with fingernail grips and 45° faces toward the center joint.
Permanent snap joint
Integral tabs lock into covered sockets to join the two halves without a separate connector.
Blended strap eyes
Rounded strap loops flow into the body, providing attachment points for a carry strap.
45° socket ceilings
Sloped roofs inside the joint pockets follow the connection-down print orientation.
