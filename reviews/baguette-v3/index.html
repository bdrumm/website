What changed

Simple internal print-in-place hinges
Smooth 2 mm radius blends join the round hinge to the inside and outside wall. The fixed hinge ends continue into the rim without exposed steps. The direct hinge retains its captured 2 mm pin and working clearances, entirely within the original case outline. Use Hinge section to inspect the transition and movement.
Two shorter latches
One latch per printed half replaces the four long tabs. The 18 mm tongues follow the case surface, with a small thumb lip for release. Each is 20 mm wide, with a 1.8 mm nominal skin and a thicker root.
Flat center faces
The large center bevel is removed. The two main segments meet at a 0.2 mm butt seam, aligned by one base sleeve and two lid keys inside the case.
Deeper hooks and reinforced catches
The inward hooks extend 1.6 mm, up from 0.65 mm. They engage behind thicker retaining ledges with about 1.1 mm of measured overlap and 3.7 mm of material behind each recess. A hidden exit bevel clears the hook during opening while retaining the full holding shoulder. Use Catch section to inspect the hook and ledge.

Assembly decision to review: the three internal joiners are bonding parts. This revision requires a compatible adhesive after a dry fit; it does not retain the original snap-together center coupling.

Print & fit review

  • 48 geometry checks passed.
  • Both latches resist an unreleased lift and clear the catches when released.
  • Every individual shell and joiner is one watertight solid.
  • Continuous 0–100° released rotation checked with a conservative clearance bound, including joiners.
  • 62 mm bore and 600 mm capsule clearance retained.
  • Both main segments fit within a 300 × 320 × 325 mm envelope, including an 8 mm brim.

Both main segments slice successfully. Together, the offline estimates are about 29.0 hours and 912 g, before the joiners and fit coupons.

Prepared for a Bambu H2C, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and PLA. Local supports are required beneath latch undercuts; keep support out of the hinge bearings.

Start with the hinge, latch, and joint coupons. Use the joiners supplied with V3.5. The default hinge coupon reproduces the current bearing; 0.3 and 0.5 mm circular-bore variants bracket the 0.4 mm minimum radial gap. The 100° range is a reviewed travel limit, without a built-in hard stop. Test the new latch coupons before a full print: the shorter tabs and deeper hooks require more flex to release. Retention force, durability and bond strength remain untested.
