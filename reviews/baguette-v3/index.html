What changed

Simple internal print-in-place hinges
Smooth 2 mm radius blends join the round hinge to the inside and outside wall. The fixed hinge ends continue into the rim without exposed steps. The direct hinge retains its captured 2 mm pin and working clearances, entirely within the original case outline. Use Hinge section to inspect the transition and movement.
Two shorter latches
One latch per printed half replaces the four long tabs. The 18 mm tongues follow the case surface, with a small thumb lip for release. Each is 20 mm wide, with a 1.8 mm nominal skin and a thicker root.
Integrated permanent snap joint
The loose sleeves are removed. A 12 mm collar printed into section A slides into section B and locks with eight one-way tabs—four on the base and four on the lid. The outside retains its 0.2 mm butt seam. Use Center joint and Joint section to inspect the integral connection.
Deeper hooks and reinforced catches
The inward hooks extend 1.6 mm, up from 0.65 mm. They engage behind thicker retaining ledges with about 1.1 mm of measured overlap and 3.7 mm of material behind each recess. A hidden exit bevel clears the hook during opening while retaining the full holding shoulder. Use Catch section to inspect the hook and ledge.

Shallower ribs: nominal inward depth is reduced from 4 mm to 1.8 mm, rib width from 3.2 mm to 2.4 mm, and the support ramp from 6 mm to 3 mm. All six rib stations retain their connection to the shell.

Print & fit review

  • 68 geometry checks passed.
  • Both latches resist an unreleased lift and clear the catches when released.
  • Every shell is one watertight solid; there are no separate joiners.
  • Continuous 0–100° released rotation checked with a conservative clearance bound, including the integral center joints.
  • 62 mm bore and 600 mm capsule clearance retained.
  • Both main segments fit within a 300 × 320 × 325 mm envelope, including an 8 mm brim.

Both main segments slice successfully. Together, the offline estimates are about 30.4 hours and 947 g, before the fit coupons.

Prepared for a Bambu H2C, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and PLA. Local supports are required beneath latch undercuts; keep support out of the hinge bearings.

Print the joint coupons before assembling the full case. The snap joint is intended to stay locked: it has no release feature, and separation may damage the tabs. Align both base and lid joints at the same hinge angle, then press along the length with even support. No separate connector or adhesive is required. Test hinge and latch coupons too; the 100° opening range has no built-in hard stop. Printed fit, retention force and durability remain untested.
