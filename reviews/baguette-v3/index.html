What changed

A smoothly blended outer lip
The raised band curves into the shell and latch face, replacing the hard ledges with a smooth transition. The side openings stay clear, and the internal catch shoulders retain their locking shape.
Rounded strap attachments
The strap eyes have curved edges and blended roots, replacing the stepped layers around the attachment. The holes stay open and retain a 3 mm outer web. Use Strap to inspect the new geometry.
Fingernail catch inside the grip
A new shallow scoop opens into the underside of the projecting grip. It leaves a front lip for a fingernail to catch, alongside the existing recess in the shell below. Use Latch and Catch section to inspect it.
Blended joint and solid snap roots
The center attachment tapers smoothly into the inner shell over 30 mm, removing the abrupt ledge. Half A’s tabs extend directly from the solid shell with no surrounding inset channel. Longer tongues latch into matching covered blind sockets in Half B.

All internal ribs removed: all six hoops and their support ramps are omitted from the print model. The reinforced cavity and blended center attachment form one continuous inner shell.

Print & fit review

  • 120 geometry checks passed.
  • Both latches resist an unreleased lift and clear the catches when released.
  • Every shell is one watertight solid; there are no separate joiners.
  • Continuous 0–100° released rotation checked with a conservative clearance bound, including the integral center joints.
  • 62 mm bore and 600 mm capsule clearance retained.
  • Both main segments fit within a 300 × 320 × 325 mm envelope, including an 8 mm brim.

Both main segments slice successfully. Together, the offline estimates are about 29.5 hours and 982 g, before the fit coupons.

Prepared for a Bambu H2C, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and PLA. Local supports are required beneath latch undercuts; keep support out of the hinge bearings.

Print the joint coupons before assembling the full case. The snap joint is intended to stay locked: it has no release feature, and separation may damage the tabs. Align both base and lid joints at the same hinge angle, then press along the length with even support. No separate connector or adhesive is required. Test hinge and latch coupons too; the 100° opening range has no built-in hard stop. Printed fit, retention force and durability remain untested.
