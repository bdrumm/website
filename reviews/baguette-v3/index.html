What changed

Simple internal print-in-place hinges
One short hinge in each printed half, with two fixed ears, one moving barrel, and a captured 2 mm pin. The 5.6 mm barrels stay inside the original case outline. The large exterior interlocking sections and long internal reinforcement tube are removed.
Longer, recessed latches
Four tapered 26 mm tongues use shallow lead-in ramps, a positive retaining shoulder, and a finger lip for release. The closed latches are unloaded in the CAD model.
Flat center faces
The large center bevel is removed. The two main segments meet at a 0.2 mm butt seam, aligned by one base sleeve and two lid keys inside the case.
Compact mounts and a relieved inside rim
Small internal mounts and a tapered rim let the lid open through the reviewed 100° range. There is no exterior hinge spine. The ribs retain their cleaned-up connections to the shell.

Assembly decision to review: the three internal joiners are bonding parts. This revision requires a compatible adhesive after a dry fit; it does not retain the original snap-together center coupling.

Print & fit review

  • 27 geometry checks passed.
  • Every individual shell and joiner is one watertight solid.
  • Released opening sweep checked every 1° from 0–100°, including joiners.
  • 62 mm bore and 600 mm capsule clearance retained.
  • Both main segments fit within a 300 × 320 × 325 mm envelope, including an 8 mm brim.

Both main segments slice successfully. Together, the offline estimates are about 30.9 hours and 948 g, before the joiners and fit coupons.

Prepared for a Bambu H2C, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers and PLA. Local supports are required beneath latch undercuts; keep support out of the hinge bearings.

Start with the hinge, latch, and joint coupons. Use the updated joiners supplied with V3.2. The default hinge coupon reproduces the current bearing; 0.3 and 0.5 mm circular-bore variants bracket the 0.4 mm minimum radial gap. The 100° range is a reviewed travel limit, without a built-in hard stop. Fit, opening force, durability, and bond strength still need physical testing.
